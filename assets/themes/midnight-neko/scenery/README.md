# Moonlit garden sprites

Built-in imagegen assets in Saya's plum-black, burgundy and silver palette.
The garden is a quiet transparent edge overlay. Bat and particle sprites use
128 × 128 RGBA canvases with proportional silhouettes for small-scale rendering.
No sprite intercepts input. The silver arrowhead has a shallow rear notch and no
shaft; its hotspot stays at the tip. Clickable controls use a matching silver
compact 27-pixel gauntlet with a raised index finger, burgundy cuff and crimson gem; the fingertip
is its hotspot. Native text, resize and other specialized cursors remain intact.

The narrow arrowhead has a 17 × 28 logical-pixel silhouette with silver bevels
and a small crimson inset. Both cursors keep a thin plum-black contrast rim.

The estate uses three cloud silhouettes, including `cloud-bank.png`, generated with
built-in imagegen from the neighboring `cloud-variant.png` style reference.
The full prompt is in `cloud-bank.prompt.txt`. Large scenery textures are fitted
to a 1024-pixel maximum side when loaded. Four cool blue-violet mist layers drift,
ripple independently and fade at their edges. Sixteen bats roam and roost.
