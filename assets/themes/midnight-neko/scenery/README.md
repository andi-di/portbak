# Moonlit garden sprites

Built-in imagegen assets in Saya's plum-black, burgundy and silver palette.
The garden is a quiet transparent edge overlay. Bat and particle sprites use
128 × 128 RGBA canvases with proportional silhouettes for small-scale rendering.
No sprite intercepts input. The silver arrowhead has a shallow rear notch and no
shaft; its hotspot stays at the tip. Clickable controls use a matching silver
gauntlet with a raised index finger, burgundy cuff and crimson gem; the fingertip
is its hotspot. Native text, resize and other specialized cursors remain intact.
